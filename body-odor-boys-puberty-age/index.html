When Do Boys Start to Get Body Odor?

By The Growth Path Editorial Team · Reviewed November 2026

One day his room or his shoes smell different. Body odor is often one of the first signs that his body is gearing up for puberty. Here is what it means and what to do.

The short answer

Body odor often starts between about 8 and 11. It is driven by adrenal hormones that begin rising before true puberty, so it can show up a year or more before other changes. It is normal and usually one of the first signs that his body is gearing up for puberty.

Hygiene that actually helps

  • A daily shower, especially after sports, with soap under the arms and on the feet
  • Deodorant or antiperspirant. Both are fine for kids once odor starts.
  • Clean socks and underwear every day, and shoes that get a chance to dry out
  • Washing sports gear and practice clothes after each use

Many boys do not notice their own smell. A matter-of-fact talk works better than teasing.

When to ask his pediatrician

  • Body odor before age 8. Often harmless, but worth mentioning.
  • Body odor along with pubic hair, acne, fast growth or a deepening voice before age 9. See early puberty signs in boys.
  • A strong, unusual odor that is not from sweat, such as a sweet or fruity smell.

Frequently asked questions

At what age do boys start to get body odor?

Often between about 8 and 11. It is driven by adrenal hormones that start rising before true puberty, so it can show up a year or more before other changes.

Does body odor mean puberty has started?

Not exactly. Body odor comes from adrenal hormones, while true puberty in boys starts with the testicles enlarging. The two often overlap, but body odor can come first.

Does body odor mean a growth spurt is coming?

It is an early clue. The main growth spurt in boys usually comes later, often a year or two after puberty begins.

When is body odor too early?

Body odor alone before age 8 is often harmless, but mention it to his pediatrician. Body odor with pubic hair, fast growth, acne or a deepening voice before age 9 should be checked for early puberty.
