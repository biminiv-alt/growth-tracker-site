Can You Help Your Son Grow Taller? What Actually Works

By The Growth Path Editorial Team · Reviewed November 2026

Every parent of a shorter boy has typed this into a search bar. The honest answer is that you cannot add inches beyond his genes, but you can make sure nothing holds him back.

The short answer

Genes decide most of how tall a boy will be. What you can do is make sure nothing gets in the way of him reaching that height: enough sleep, enough food, and a doctor who checks his growth over time.

To see the range his genes point to, use the family height calculator.

What actually helps

  • Sleep. Most growth hormone is released in deep sleep. Teens need about 8 to 10 hours. See sleep and height in teen boys.
  • Enough food, especially protein. Growing boys, and active ones above all, need a lot of calories. Too little food over a long time can slow growth. See nutrition for growing boys.
  • Calcium and vitamin D. These build strong bones. Milk, yogurt and cheese help, but they do not make a well-fed boy taller. See does milk help boys grow taller and vitamin D and growth.
  • Being active. Sport and play are good for his bones and his confidence. Lifting weights with good form does not stunt growth. See does lifting weights stunt growth.
  • Checking for hidden problems. Conditions like celiac disease or low thyroid can quietly slow growth, and treating them lets growth catch up.

What does not work

  • "Grow taller" supplements and pills. None have been shown to work in healthy kids, and some contain hormones or stimulants.
  • Stretching programs, hanging and insoles sold as height boosters. They do not lengthen bones.
  • Extra milk or protein shakes for a boy who already eats well. More than enough does not add height.
  • Picking a sport for height. Basketball does not make boys taller. See sports and height.

When to see the doctor

Talk to his pediatrician if he is growing less than about 2 inches a year before puberty, if he is dropping across percentile lines on his growth chart, if he is far shorter than his family height suggests, or if there are no signs of puberty by 14. Bring dated measurements. See what to track before the visit.

Frequently asked questions

Can a boy increase his height naturally?

A healthy boy cannot grow taller than his genes allow. Good sleep, enough food and protein, and treating any medical problem help him reach his full potential.

Do height growth supplements work?

No supplement has been shown to make a healthy child taller. Some contain hormones or stimulants and can be harmful. Talk to his pediatrician before giving him any supplement.

Does stretching or hanging make you taller?

No. Stretching and hanging do not lengthen bones. Good posture can make him look a little taller, but it does not change his actual height.

Does basketball make you taller?

No. Tall kids are drawn to basketball, but playing it does not make a boy taller. Being active is healthy, whatever the sport.

When is growth hormone used?

Growth hormone is prescribed by a specialist for specific diagnosed conditions, such as growth hormone deficiency. It is not a treatment for a healthy boy who is simply on the short side.
