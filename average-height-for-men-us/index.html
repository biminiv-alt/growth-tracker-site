Average Height for Men in the US

By The Growth Path Editorial Team · Reviewed November 2026

When parents worry about a short son, the real question is often what he will be as an adult. Here is the adult benchmark.

The short answer

The average adult man in the US is about 5'9" (175 cm), based on CDC national health survey data. The average adult woman is about 5'3.5". Most men fall within a few inches of the average.

Rough guide to adult heights

Adult heightRoughly
Under 5'5"Shorter than about 9 in 10 US men
5'7" to 5'11"The middle range, where most men are
6'0" and overTaller than about 5 in 6 US men

These are rounded and shift a little by birth year and background.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average height for a man in the US?

About 5'9" (175 cm) for men aged 20 and over, based on CDC national health survey data.

What is considered short for a man?

There is no medical cutoff for adults. Around 5'5" and under is shorter than roughly 9 in 10 US men.

What is the average height for an 18-year-old boy?

About 5'9" (176 cm) on CDC growth charts, close to the adult average, since most boys are nearly done growing by 18.

How can I tell how tall my son will be?

His parents' heights are the best single guide. Add both parents' heights, divide by 2, and add about 2.5 inches for a boy.
