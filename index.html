What a pediatrician actually reads

This is a sample of the one-page Appointment Brief included in the $49 Kit. Doctors read it in under 30 seconds. It changes the conversation from "he's tracking fine" to "here is exactly what I want to understand."

Chief concern

I want to understand my son's growth pattern and know what, if anything, warrants further evaluation.

The data

  • Height: 4'6" (54.0 in). BMI: 15.8.
  • Current CDC percentile for age and sex: 12th.
  • 12-month growth velocity: 1.7 in.
  • Mid-parental target range: 5'6" to 6'0".
  • Family history of late puberty: yes (father).

The three questions I want to prioritize today

  • Does his 1.7-inch 12-month velocity fall within the normal range for a 10-year-old boy?
  • Given the family history and the gap between his current percentile and mid-parental prediction, would you consider a bone age X-ray?
  • At what percentile drop or velocity change would you refer to a pediatric endocrinologist?

What we have already tried

  • Consistent 9.5-hour sleep window since March.
  • No dietary restrictions. Eats well.
  • No prior specialist consults.
