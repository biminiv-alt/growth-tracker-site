The Stages of Puberty in Boys, and When He Grows Fastest

By The Growth Path Editorial Team · Reviewed November 2026

Doctors track puberty in five stages, named after the pediatrician James Tanner. Knowing roughly which stage your son is in tells you more about his growth than his birthday does.

The short answer

Puberty in boys runs through five stages, usually over 3 to 5 years. It most often starts somewhere between 9 and 14, and the fastest growth year usually comes in the middle stages, about 2 years after it begins. A boy who has not reached that middle stage yet usually has his biggest growth still ahead.

The five stages at a glance

StageWhat is happeningGrowth
1Before puberty. No changes yet.Steady, about 2 inches a year
2Testicles start to enlarge, a little pubic hair. Usually 9 to 14.Still steady, sometimes a small dip just before the spurt
3More pubic hair, body starts to change, voice may start to crack.Growth speeds up
4Voice deepens, underarm hair, more muscle.Often the fastest year, 3 to 4 inches or more
5Adult body. Facial hair keeps filling in for years.Slowing, then stopping

Stages blur into each other, and boys do not all go through every change in the same order. Doctors judge the stage at a physical exam.

Where the growth spurt falls

Unlike girls, who grow fastest early in puberty, boys grow fastest in the middle to late stages. That is why a boy can be in early puberty and still look small next to his friends. See peak growth velocity in boys and how much boys grow after puberty starts.

Clues you can see at home

  • Early: bigger shoe size, a growing appetite, body odor. See feet grow before height.
  • Middle: voice cracking, fast height gain, clumsiness. See when boys' voices change.
  • Late: a deeper voice, shaving starting, height gain slowing. See growth after he starts shaving.

When to ask his pediatrician

  • Signs of puberty before age 9. See early puberty signs.
  • No signs of puberty by age 14. Often a late bloomer, but worth a check. See is my son a late bloomer.
  • Puberty that starts and then seems to stall for more than a couple of years.

Frequently asked questions

What are the Tanner stages?

They are five stages doctors use to describe how far along puberty is, from stage 1 (before puberty) to stage 5 (adult). Each stage is judged from physical changes, not age.

What is the first sign of puberty in boys?

Testicles getting bigger is usually the first sign, often around age 11 or 12, though anywhere from 9 to 14 is normal. Pubic hair usually follows a few months later.

At what stage do boys grow the fastest?

The fastest growth usually comes in the middle of puberty, around stages 3 and 4, often about 2 years after puberty begins. On average that is around age 13 to 14.

How long does puberty take in boys?

Usually about 3 to 5 years from the first signs to an adult body, though it varies a lot between boys.
