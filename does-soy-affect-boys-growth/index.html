Does Soy Affect Boys' Growth or Puberty?

By The Growth Path Editorial Team · Reviewed November 2026

Some parents worry that soy milk or tofu could change a boy's hormones or growth. Here is what studies have found.

The short answer

At normal amounts in food, research has not shown that soy changes boys' height or the timing of their puberty. Soy is a complete protein, and soy milk with added calcium and vitamin D can stand in for cow's milk.

Why parents worry

Soy contains isoflavones, plant compounds that look a little like estrogen. They act far more weakly than the body's own hormones. Studies in children eating normal soy foods, and in boys who had soy infant formula, have not found clear effects on growth or puberty.

Frequently asked questions

Does soy stunt boys' growth?

Research has not shown that normal amounts of soy in food affect boys' height.

Does soy cause early puberty or other hormone changes in boys?

Studies have not found that normal soy intake changes the timing of puberty in boys. Soy contains plant compounds called isoflavones, which act much more weakly than human hormones.

Is soy milk OK for a growing boy?

Yes, as part of a balanced diet. Choose a version with added calcium and vitamin D if it replaces cow's milk.

How much soy is too much?

Normal servings like soy milk, tofu or edamame are fine. Supplements with concentrated isoflavones are a different thing and not needed for kids.
