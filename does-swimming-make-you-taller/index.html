Does Swimming Make You Taller?

By The Growth Path Editorial Team · Reviewed November 2026

Elite swimmers are often very tall, so it is natural to wonder if the pool helped. Here is what is going on.

The short answer

No, swimming does not make a child taller. Elite swimmers are often tall because height helps in the pool, so tall kids are more likely to stick with it and win. That is selection, not swimming changing how they grow.

Why swimmers look tall

  • Longer arms and legs mean more reach per stroke.
  • Coaches and clubs tend to keep the kids who are winning, and size helps.
  • Good posture from training can make swimmers stand a little straighter.

Frequently asked questions

Does swimming increase height?

No. There is no good evidence that swimming makes children grow taller than their genes allow.

Why are swimmers so tall?

Height, long arms and big hands are advantages in the pool, so tall people are more likely to succeed and stay in the sport.

Is swimming good for a growing boy?

Yes. It builds fitness and strength with little strain on growing joints, and it can help him sleep well, which supports normal growth.
