Does Jumping Make You Taller?

By The Growth Path Editorial Team · Reviewed November 2026

Jump rope and basketball are often sold as ways to grow taller. Here is what jumping actually does for a growing boy.

The short answer

No. Jumping does not make bones grow longer. His adult height comes mostly from genes, with good food and sleep helping him reach it. Jumping is still good for him, because it builds stronger bones.

Frequently asked questions

Does jumping make you taller?

No. Height is set mostly by genes and by the growth plates near the ends of the bones. Jumping does not make those plates grow faster.

Why are basketball players tall?

Mostly because tall kids are picked for basketball and stick with it. Playing the sport does not cause the height.

Is jumping good for growing kids?

Yes. Jumping and other impact activity build stronger, denser bones during the teen years, when most adult bone strength is laid down.

Can jumping hurt his growth plates?

Normal play and sports do not harm growth plates. Pain at the knee or heel during a spurt is common and worth a check if it lasts.
