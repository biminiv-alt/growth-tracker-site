How Tall Will a 5-Foot 12-Year-Old Boy Be?

By The Growth Path Editorial Team · Reviewed November 2026

Five feet at 12 is a little taller than average. Here is what that usually means for his adult height, and what can change it.

The short answer

Around 5'10" to 5'11", if he stays on the same line on the CDC chart. At 12, 5'0" is about the 66th percentile. Following that percentile forward gives roughly the heights below.

Frequently asked questions

How tall will a 5-foot 12-year-old boy be?

If he stays on the same CDC percentile, about 5'10" to 5'11" as an adult. That is an estimate, not a promise.

Is 5 feet tall for a 12-year-old boy?

A little. 5'0" at 12 is around the 66th percentile on CDC charts, above the average of about 4'11".

Could he end up shorter than that?

Yes. Boys who are tall at 12 because they started puberty early often finish sooner and land closer to average.

What is the best way to predict his adult height?

Combine his percentile line with his parents' heights. A bone-age X-ray adds the most when his doctor thinks it is needed.
