Growth Check

A 10-question snapshot.

Answer honestly. Nothing is diagnostic. At the end you'll see where he sits, what to monitor, and what to do next.

Step 1 of 10

1. How old is your son?

Enter his age in years (decimals okay).

2. What is his current height?

The last measurement you have.

3. One prior height (optional).

If you have an earlier measurement, it helps us estimate growth velocity. Skip if unknown.

4. Mother's height.

This helps us calculate a family-context range.

5. Father's height.

Second half of the family-context calculation.

6. How is his puberty or development timing compared to peers?

Your best read. It's fine if you're unsure.

7. What prompted your concern?

Choose the one that fits best.

8. Has size affected sports, confidence or social situations?

Whatever you're noticing.

9. What are you most uncertain about?

This shapes the plan we show you.

10. What have you already done?

Check anything that applies.
