Early Puberty in Boys: Signs and What It Means for Height

By The Growth Path Editorial Team · Reviewed November 2026

Most boys start puberty between 9 and 14. When signs show up earlier, it is worth a doctor visit, partly because of what early puberty can do to his final height.

The short answer

In boys, signs of puberty before age 9 are considered early, and doctors call it precocious puberty. It is much less common in boys than in girls, and in boys it is more likely to have an underlying medical cause. That is why early signs are always worth a pediatrician visit.

Signs to watch for before age 9

  • Enlarging testicles or penis
  • Pubic, underarm or facial hair
  • A deepening voice
  • Acne or adult body odor
  • A sudden growth spurt that puts him well ahead of his classmates

For the normal ages and order of these signs, see when do boys start puberty.

Why early puberty matters for height

Boys with early puberty often shoot up and look tall for their age. The catch is that the same hormones that speed up growth also make the growth plates close sooner. Growth can stop early, and without treatment some boys end up shorter as adults than their family height would suggest.

More on this in when growth plates close in boys.

How doctors check it

  • Growth chart. A sudden jump across percentile lines is a key clue.
  • Exam. The pediatrician checks his stage of puberty.
  • Bone age x-ray. Shows whether his bones are maturing faster than his age. See bone age x-ray.
  • Blood tests and sometimes a brain scan. A pediatric endocrinologist uses these to look for a cause.

How it is treated

If puberty is moving fast or has a cause that needs care, a pediatric endocrinologist may use medicine that pauses puberty until a more typical age, and treat any underlying problem. Many boys then go on to reach a normal adult height. See when to see a pediatric endocrinologist.

Frequently asked questions

What age is early puberty in boys?

Signs of puberty before age 9 in a boy are considered early. Doctors call this precocious puberty.

What are the signs of early puberty in boys?

Enlarging testicles or penis, pubic, underarm or facial hair, a deepening voice, acne, adult body odor, and a sudden growth spurt before age 9.

Does early puberty make boys shorter?

It can. Boys with early puberty are often tall for their age at first, but their growth plates close sooner, so they may stop growing early and end up shorter than expected if it is not treated.

How is early puberty treated?

A pediatric endocrinologist looks for a cause and may use medicine that pauses puberty until a more typical age. Whether treatment is needed depends on the cause and how fast puberty is moving.
