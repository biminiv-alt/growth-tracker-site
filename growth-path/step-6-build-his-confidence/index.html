Step 6 of 7

Build HIS confidence.

You cannot make him taller today. You can build the confidence that carries him regardless of how tall he ends up.

Age changes everything.

The questions at 8 are different from 12 are different from 16. At 8, he is noticing. At 12, he is comparing constantly, and locker rooms make it worse. At 16, he is deciding what kind of man he wants to be, and height is one input, not the whole story.

The scripts have to match the age. What lands as reassuring at 8 sounds patronizing at 14. Step 6 gives you the words for each stage.

Inside this step:

  • Age-by-age scripts for the hard conversations
  • What to do when he sits the bench (and what NOT to do)
  • The research on shorter boys who thrive as adults (spoiler: height is one variable of many)
Confidence is the compounding asset that outlasts height.
