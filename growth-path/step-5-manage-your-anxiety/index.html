Step 5 of 7

Manage YOUR anxiety.

The 2am spiral. The comparison at the school pickup. The tightness when he asks "am I ever going to be tall?" This is the mom-facing step.

Why anxiety compounds.

The loop is predictable. You watch him at practice. You compare him to the taller kid. You catastrophize the next ten years in about forty seconds. Then you project that fear back onto him, in your tone, in the questions you ask, in the way your hand tightens on his shoulder.

He feels it. He does not need to know what you were thinking. Kids read the room, and the room is you.

Inside this step:

  • The comparison-trap script (how to interrupt it in the moment)
  • What to say when your partner minimizes it
  • The 10-minute daily practice that anxious moms of shorter boys swear by
You cannot help him thrive if you are the most anxious person in the room.

"I was the problem I kept trying to fix in him. Once I named that, everything else got easier."
