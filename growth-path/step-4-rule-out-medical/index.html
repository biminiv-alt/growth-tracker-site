Rule out the medical stuff.

You saw the range in Step 2. You know what can influence height from Step 3. Now the biggest question: is there something medically going on that a workup would catch?

What a pediatric endocrinologist actually does.

A pediatric endocrinologist is a doctor who specializes in kids' hormones and growth. The evaluation is not scary. It is a conversation, a hand X-ray to check bone age, a blood draw to look at a few key markers, and sometimes a growth-hormone stimulation test if the first round of results points that way.

Most families walk out with reassurance and a plan. Some walk out with a diagnosis that changes the trajectory. Either way, you stop guessing.
