Step 3 of 7

Understand what can influence height.

You have heard supplement companies promise inches. You have heard doctors say "his height is his height." Both are wrong.

Here is what science actually knows, said in plain English, with nothing to sell you.

The genetics floor. Be honest.

Roughly 60 to 80 percent of your son's adult height is heritable. His DNA sets the range. Nothing on the internet is going to change the range itself.

This sounds like a downer. It is not. Setting the expectation honestly is part of hope. When you know the range is real and finite, you can stop searching for a miracle and start focusing on what actually moves him toward the top of that range instead of the bottom.

The goal is not "outrun genetics." The goal is "reach the top of his genetic range instead of the bottom." That is where the real inches live, and that is not nothing.

Real medical interventions. The biggest single factor, when indicated.

This is the piece with the largest evidence base, and it is the piece most parents get the least honest information about. Growth hormone therapy is not a cosmetic drug. For boys with a documented indication, it works.

  • GH for CDGP Constitutional delay of growth and puberty. Documented 2 to 4 inches added to adult height when properly indicated. Requires a diagnosis, a bone age X-ray, and a pediatric endocrinologist making the call.
  • GH for ISS Idiopathic short stature. FDA approved. Real. Requires a workup that rules out other causes and confirms he is below the treatment threshold.
  • GH for growth hormone deficiency Restores the growth trajectory his body was supposed to have. Diagnosed with stimulation testing, treated with daily injections, tracked over years.

This is not for every boy on the shorter side. It is for boys whose workup indicates it. Step 4 covers the workup itself: when to ask for a referral, what tests actually happen, and what a diagnosis looks like on paper.

Sleep. The underrated lever.

Growth hormone releases in pulses, and the biggest pulses happen during deep sleep. A boy who is chronically sleep-deprived, especially through puberty, may not reach his genetic potential. This is not a wellness slogan. It is endocrinology.

The intervention is free. Consistent bedtime. Cool, dark room. Phones out of the bedroom. Enough hours that he is not dragging himself out of bed every morning. If he is getting six hours on school nights, this is your first move, not a supplement.

Nutrition timing during growth spurts.

Adequate protein and adequate calories during puberty matter. That is the sentence. Not "eat this superfood." Not "here is a shake to buy." Enough of the right things at the right time.

The kid who under-eats through his peak growth years does not get those inches back. The kid who eats protein at every meal, keeps calories up, and does not skip breakfast is giving his body the raw material to finish the job his DNA set out to do.

What does NOT work. Be direct.

The internet is a graveyard of expensive nothing. Save your money.

None of the following add adult height: Height-increase supplements. Height-increase exercises. Stretching routines. Hanging upside down. Special insoles. "Grow taller" powders, teas, or so-called peptides sold to parents. Every one of these is a whole category, not a specific brand. All of them.

If a product's marketing implies it will make your son taller, and it is not a prescription medication from a pediatric endocrinologist, it will not make your son taller. That includes the well-packaged, well-reviewed ones.

What this actually means for your son.

Genetics sets the range. Medical intervention when warranted, plus sleep, plus nutrition, can help him reach the top of that range instead of the bottom. In real terms, that is often 2 to 4 inches. That is meaningful. That is the whole game.

Not a miracle. Not a scam. A real, evidence-based path.
