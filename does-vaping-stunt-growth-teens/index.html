Does Vaping Stunt Growth in Teens?

By The Growth Path Editorial Team · Reviewed November 2026

Teens hear that vaping stunts growth. The honest answer is more specific: the bigger risks are to his brain, lungs and sleep. Here is what the evidence says.

The short answer

There is no strong evidence that vaping on its own makes a teen shorter. His adult height is mostly set by genetics, nutrition, sleep and overall health. But that is not the same as harmless. The real concerns for a growing teen are his brain, his lungs, and his sleep.

What nicotine does in the teen years

  • Addiction. Many vapes deliver a lot of nicotine, and teens can become dependent quickly.
  • Brain development. US health agencies warn that nicotine can harm the developing brain, including attention, learning, mood and impulse control. The brain keeps developing into the mid-20s.
  • Lungs. Smoking during the teen years is known to slow lung growth. Research on vaping is newer, but vapor contains chemicals that irritate the airways.

The indirect effects on growth

  • Sleep. Nicotine is a stimulant. Less sleep and later sleep matter while he is growing. See sleep and height in teenage boys.
  • Appetite. Nicotine can blunt hunger, and growing boys need a lot of food. See why teen boys are always hungry.
  • Sports. Coughing, wheezing and shortness of breath can get in the way of training.

Talking to him about it

  • Ask before you lecture. What does he know, and what are his friends doing?
  • Lead with what he cares about now: sports, sleep, money, feeling in control, not a distant health risk.
  • Stay calm if he admits it. Teens who feel they can be honest are easier to help.
  • If he wants to quit, his pediatrician can help, and there are free quit programs designed for teens.

Frequently asked questions

Does vaping make you shorter?

There is no strong evidence that vaping by itself reduces a teen's adult height. Height is mostly set by genetics, nutrition, sleep and overall health.

What does nicotine do to a teenager?

Nicotine is highly addictive, and US health agencies warn that it can harm the parts of the developing brain that control attention, learning, mood and impulse control. The brain keeps developing into the mid-20s.

Does vaping affect lung growth?

Smoking during the teen years is known to slow lung growth. Research on vaping and lungs is newer, but vapor contains chemicals that irritate the airways, and many teens who vape report coughing and wheezing.

Can vaping affect sleep and appetite?

Yes. Nicotine is a stimulant that can make it harder to fall asleep and can blunt appetite, and both sleep and good nutrition matter while a teen is growing.
