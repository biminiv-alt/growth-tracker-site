What "average" means here

The average (50th percentile) height for a 14-year-old boy in the U.S. is 163.2 cm (5' 4.3"). That means about half of 14-year-old boys are taller than this, and half are shorter. It does not mean anything is wrong with a 14-year-old who is taller or shorter, every percentile from about the 3rd to the 97th falls within the range of typical variation.

Why boys the same age can be very different heights

At 14, most boys are somewhere in puberty. Some have already had their peak growth spurt; others are just entering it. This is one of the widest years for height variation between boys the same age.

Peak height velocity (the fastest growth year in puberty) hits at wildly different ages across boys. A 14-year-old who peaked at 13 may only have 2 inches left; a 14-year-old who peaks at 15 has 5+ inches ahead.

When to talk to a pediatrician

Height alone is rarely diagnostic. What pediatricians look for at age 14:

  • No signs of puberty by 14 is worth a pediatrician visit, this is the age it starts to become a real clinical question, not just late.
  • Height below the 3rd percentile with no family explanation.
  • Sustained downward percentile crossing.
  • Growth velocity below 2 inches per year during what should be peak growth.

If any of these apply, that is a conversation to bring to his next visit, ideally with 6-12 months of measurements in hand.

Frequently asked questions

Is my 14-year-old short?

5' 0" (152 cm) at age 14 is around the 10th percentile. Shorter, but often just late puberty timing, worth a conversation with the pediatrician if no signs of puberty are showing.

What if my 14-year-old is tall?

5' 8" (173 cm) at age 14 is around the 90th percentile, tall, typically driven by earlier puberty timing and family height.

Do boys still grow after 14?

Yes, most 14-year-old boys have 3-4 more years of growth ahead. Some finish by 16, others grow into their early 20s.

How much should a 14-year-old grow in a year?

3-4 inches per year during peak growth velocity; 1-2 inches per year in the tail years of puberty.
