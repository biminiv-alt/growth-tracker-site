When Do Boys Get Armpit Hair?

By The Growth Path Editorial Team · Reviewed November 2026

Underarm hair is one of the easier puberty signs for parents to notice. Here is when it usually shows up and what it means for his height.

The short answer

Most boys get armpit hair around 12 to 14. It usually shows up about midway through puberty, after other early changes, and often near his fastest growth year. Anywhere from about 10 to 16 can be normal.

What it says about his height

If underarm hair has just appeared, he is often in or near his fastest growth year. If he has a full set and is shaving, most of his height gain is usually behind him. See when boys grow facial hair and growth after his voice changes.

Helping him with the practical side

  • Keep deodorant in the bathroom without making a big deal of it.
  • Daily showers after sports help a lot.

Frequently asked questions

At what age do boys get armpit hair?

Most boys get underarm hair around 12 to 14, though anywhere from about 10 to 16 can be normal.

Does armpit hair come before or after the growth spurt?

It usually appears around the middle of puberty, often near or just after his fastest growth year.

Is it normal to have no armpit hair at 15?

It can be, especially in late developers. If there are no signs of puberty at all by 14, a doctor visit is a good idea.

Does armpit hair mean he will start smelling?

Body odor often starts a bit earlier, but more hair can trap sweat. Daily showers and deodorant help.
