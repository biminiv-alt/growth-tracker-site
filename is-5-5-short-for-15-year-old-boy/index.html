Is 5'5" Short for a 15-Year-Old Boy?

By The Growth Path Editorial Team · Reviewed November 2026

At 15, boys range widely, because some are nearly done and others have barely started. Here is where 5'5" falls and what to look at next.

The short answer

A little, but it is normal. At 15, a boy who is 5'5" is around the 26th percentile on CDC charts. About 1 in 4 boys his age are shorter. The average at 15 is about 5'7". Many 15-year-olds still have real growth ahead.

Frequently asked questions

Is 5'5" short for a 15-year-old boy?

It is a bit below average but well within the normal range. On CDC charts, 5'5" at 15 is around the 26th percentile.

What is the average height for a 15-year-old boy?

About 5'7" (170 cm) at his 15th birthday on CDC charts, rising to about 5'8.5" by 16.

How much will a 15-year-old boy still grow?

It depends on where he is in puberty. A boy still early in puberty may grow 4 inches or more; one who is shaving with an adult voice may have an inch or two left.

Is 5'5" short for a 16-year-old boy?

At 16, 5'5" is around the 13th percentile. That is still normal, but his growth speed and family heights matter more.
