How Much Do Boys Grow at 14?

By The Growth Path Editorial Team · Reviewed November 2026

Fourteen is the peak growth year for many boys. Here is how much is typical, and why two boys the same age can grow very different amounts.

The short answer

About 2.5 to 3 inches between 14 and 15 on average, from CDC charts. That average mixes boys at very different stages. A boy in the middle of his spurt often grows 3 to 4 inches that year, while a boy who has not started may grow 2 inches or less.

Frequently asked questions

How much do boys grow at 14?

On CDC charts the average boy grows about 2.5 to 3 inches between his 14th and 15th birthdays. Boys at the peak of their spurt often grow 3 to 4 inches that year.

Is 14 the peak growth year for boys?

For many boys, yes. The average boy grows fastest around 13.5 to 14, but anywhere from about 12 to 16 is normal.

My 14-year-old only grew 1 inch this year. Is that normal?

It can be, if his spurt has not started yet or is already done. If he shows no signs of puberty at 14, mention it to his doctor.

How much do boys grow after 14?

On CDC charts the average boy grows about 5 more inches between his 14th birthday and 18, and late bloomers often grow more.
