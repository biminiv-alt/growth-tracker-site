How Long Does Puberty Last in Boys?

By The Growth Path Editorial Team · Reviewed November 2026

Puberty is not one event but a stretch of several years. Here is how long it usually lasts in boys, what happens in what order, and where height fits in.

The short answer

Puberty in boys usually lasts about 3 to 5 years. It most often starts between 9 and 14 and finishes around 15 to 17. His height growth usually winds down a little after the other changes, often around 16 to 18.

What happens, in the usual order

RoughlyWhat you may notice
Year 1Early body changes, body odor, growth starting to pick up
Year 2Body hair begins, growth speeding up
Year 2 to 3Fastest growth year, voice cracking, often 3 to 4 inches in a year
Year 3 to 4Voice settles deeper, underarm and facial hair, growth slowing
Year 4 to 5Shaving, filling out, the last inch or two of height

The stages have names doctors use. See the stages of puberty in boys.

Where height fits in

Boys' biggest growth year usually comes in the middle of puberty, not at the start. That is why many boys look small at 12 or 13 and then shoot up. Once his voice has broken, most of his growth is usually behind him. See how much boys grow after their voice changes.

When to ask his doctor

  • Signs of puberty before 9
  • No signs of puberty by 14
  • Puberty that starts and then seems to stall for a long time

Frequently asked questions

How long does puberty last for boys?

Usually about 3 to 5 years from the first signs to the end, though some boys move faster or slower.

When does puberty end for boys?

Most boys finish puberty around 15 to 17. Height growth usually stops a little after, often around 16 to 18.

What is the first sign of puberty in boys?

Usually growth of the testicles, often around 11 to 12. It is easy to miss, so many parents first notice body odor or a growth spurt.

Does a late start mean puberty lasts longer?

Not necessarily. Boys who start late often move through puberty a bit faster, and many end up close to their family-height range.
