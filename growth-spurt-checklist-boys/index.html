Frequently asked questions

What are the first signs of a growth spurt in boys?

Bigger feet, a bigger appetite and body odor are often the first things parents notice, usually before he gets much taller.

How much do boys grow in a growth spurt?

At the peak, many boys grow about 3 to 4 inches in a year. The whole spurt usually lasts 2 to 3 years.

When do boys have their growth spurt?

Most often between about 12 and 15, with the fastest year around 13 to 14. Anywhere from about 11 to 16 can be normal.

Does a cracking voice mean he is done growing?

No. Voice change usually overlaps with his fastest growth. Once his voice has settled, growth starts to slow.
