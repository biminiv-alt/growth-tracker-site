Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

The Growth Path is an educational website for parents of growing boys. This page explains, in plain English, what information the site collects, why, and who handles it.

The Growth Check

The height math in the Growth Check (his percentile and family-adjusted range) is calculated in your browser. To see your results you enter your email address. When you do, we send your email address, your son's age, his height percentile and his family-adjusted range to Kit (ConvertKit), the service we use to email you your results and our weekly note on boys' growth. We do not ask for your son's name.

Email

If you give us your email, Kit stores it so we can send you your results and the weekly note. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Analytics and advertising cookies

The site uses Google Analytics, Cloudflare Web Analytics and the Meta Pixel to understand which pages are read and which ads bring visitors. These tools may set cookies or collect details such as your browser, device, pages visited and approximate location. They do not receive your Growth Check answers. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Purchases

The Growth Visit Prep Kit is sold through Gumroad. Gumroad handles your payment and card details under its own privacy policy, and we never see your card number. Gumroad shares your email address and order details with us so we can deliver the Kit and handle refunds.

Children

The Growth Path is written for parents and other adults, not for children. We do not knowingly collect information directly from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to see or delete the information we hold about you by emailing hello@thegrowthpath.app. We do not sell your email address.

Not medical advice

Nothing on this site is medical advice. Always talk to a qualified pediatric provider about your son's growth.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
