Is 5'2" Short for a 14-Year-Old Boy?

By The Growth Path Editorial Team · Reviewed November 2026

Fourteen is the age with the widest spread in boys' heights, because some have finished their spurt and others have not started. Here is where 5'2" falls.

The short answer

A bit, but it is within the normal range. At 14, a boy who is 5'2" is around the 21st percentile on CDC growth charts. The average at 14 is about 5'4.5". Many boys this height at 14 simply have not had their growth spurt yet.

Frequently asked questions

Is 5'2" short for a 14-year-old boy?

A little, but it is normal. On CDC charts 5'2" at 14 is around the 21st percentile, so about 1 in 5 boys his age are shorter.

What is the average height for a 14-year-old boy?

About 5'4.5" (164 cm) at his 14th birthday on CDC charts, rising to about 5'7" by 15.

Will my 14-year-old grow more?

Almost always. Most boys still have several inches to go at 14, and boys who have not started their spurt often have the most growth left.

Is 5'2" short for a 15-year-old boy?

At 15 it is around the 6th percentile. Still in the normal range, but worth checking his growth speed and puberty stage with his doctor.
