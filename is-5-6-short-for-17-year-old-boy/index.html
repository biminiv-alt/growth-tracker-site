Is 5'6" Short for a 17-Year-Old Boy?

By The Growth Path Editorial Team · Reviewed November 2026

By 17 most boys are close to their adult height, but late bloomers often are not. Here is where 5'6" falls and how to tell if he still has growth left.

The short answer

A bit. At 17, a boy who is 5'6" is around the 15th percentile on CDC growth charts. The average at 17 is about 5'9". Whether he grows more depends on how far through puberty he is, not on his age alone.

Frequently asked questions

Is 5'6" short for a 17-year-old boy?

A bit. On CDC charts 5'6" at 17 is around the 15th percentile, so about 1 in 7 boys his age are shorter. It is still in the normal range.

What is the average height for a 17-year-old boy?

About 5'9" (175 cm) at his 17th birthday on CDC charts, nearly the same as the average adult man.

Can a 17-year-old boy still grow?

Some can. Boys who started puberty late, or who are still getting taller each year, often add 1 to 2 inches after 17.

How do I know if my 17-year-old is done growing?

If he has grown under half an inch in the past year and his voice and facial hair are well along, he is likely close to done. An X-ray of the hand can tell for sure.
